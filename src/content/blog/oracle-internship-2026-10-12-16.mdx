This week was a little tough. With the recent layoffs and their impact on our team, the workload has been rising, and we’re facing shorter due dates as the end of the year approaches. I’m not overwhelmed by it, but it’s something to think about. Previous work has also been slowly accumulating, which means we’re facing more intense release days. I trust my team, and I know we’ll get as much work done and release as much as possible before December arrives.

## Meeting students from Tec Nacional de Apatzingán

On Friday, I also had the opportunity to give a presentation for Tec Nacional de Apatzingán. It was a fun way to meet new people, give them advice about what the enterprise world is like as an intern, and share some tips and tricks for building their C.V.

They were all C.S. students in their 7th semester. When I asked a few more technical questions, though, they didn’t answer as I expected. I want to believe it was because they were shy, but it would be concerning if it were due to a lack of technical knowledge.

Either way, I recommended some resources on data structures, roadmaps, and lots of LeetCode. I advised them to learn as much as possible on their own, which is the best way to get ahead. Oswaldo also mentioned some courses they could take for free through Oracle My Learn, which is another great resource.

## Looking ahead

Overall, although my workload has increased, I can handle it for now. I’m hoping things get much better by January. I always try to make some spare time to interact with visitors from universities, which is also a great opportunity for me to improve my public speaking skills. It’s always nice to do something different at work.
