This week felt a little more normal than the last one. There wasn’t much new development to write about, but we had a meeting to explore the direction our organization might take.

I’m still trying to understand the changes. From what I gathered, we’ll be joining other organizations as part of a larger group. It sounds interesting, but I’m not yet sure what that means for our product.

Mostly, I’m hoping I can keep working with my current team in the coming months.

## Sharing my experience with visitors

On Thursday, I joined Alfred and Vivian for a presentation to students visiting from UNIVA.

My part was a little improvised, but I enjoyed the chance to talk with the visitors and share some of my experience as an intern. It was a nice change from the usual workday.

Apparently, I’ll be helping with another student visit next week. I’m looking forward to having another opportunity to share what the internship has been like, and I’ll write about it when it happens.

## Continuing with the mentoring program

I’m also keeping up with my mentees. One asked for help putting together her CV, while the other and I have been brainstorming ideas for his academic project.

I think they’re enjoying the program as much as I am. It’s been nice to have these conversations and help them work through something specific each time we meet.

## Waiting for a clearer plan

I hope next week brings more clarity. We’ve been told that the worst of the changes is behind us and that there may not be many more organizational changes for now.

There are still practical questions to work through, though: who will take the lead, and how will we handle processes that need supervision or approval?

Things feel a little steadier than last week, even if we don’t have all the answers yet.
