This week was more exciting than the last one. I got to meet our new product lead, spent time practicing technical interviews with my mentees, and had another chance to speak with students visiting the MDC.

Our new product lead is British and has been very nice to work with. After the uncertainty of the past couple of weeks, it was good to start getting to know someone taking on that role.

## Practicing technical interviews

I’ve been doing technical mock interviews with my mentees. They’re both in their fourth semester, and we’ve found that data structures, algorithms, and live coding are areas where they could use more practice.

Working through a LeetCode-style problem after reading its description doesn’t feel very comfortable for them yet. Luckily, we still have about a month in the program to practice together and build that confidence.

I love talking about data structures and explaining how they work. I could spend hours on those conversations, and my mentees seem to enjoy them too. That makes it a pretty good arrangement for all of us.

## Speaking with visiting students

Oswaldo invited me to give an overview of the internship to students from Tecnológico Nacional de Zapopan.

I shared some of my experience, talked about building a CV, and described what it’s been like to work at Oracle as an intern. It wasn’t a large group, but they were engaged and asked thoughtful questions. That was the part I appreciated most.

It also gave me a reason to step outside my comfort zone and get back into public speaking. I like doing it, but it’s easy to forget that when I spend most of the day at a computer.

After this visit, I’m looking forward to the possibility of giving a talk or helping with a tour during Oracle Day for Tec students in November.

## Planning a home for these entries

I’m also working on moving these blog entries to a personal website. It’s taking some time, but I hope to have at least one entry there by the end of the semester.

I’m excited about having a place of my own to share them. Hopefully, I’ll have something to show soon.
