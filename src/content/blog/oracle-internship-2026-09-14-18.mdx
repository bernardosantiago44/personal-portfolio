This was a difficult week. On Monday, my manager, my buddy, and two teammates were laid off, along with two managers above mine. In my organization alone, about 40 to 45 people lost their positions.

The first couple of days were hard to get through. It was difficult to concentrate, and I wasn’t the only person on the team feeling that way.

## Working through the uncertainty

There was a lot to rearrange. We welcomed a teammate who had lost the rest of his team, while trying to understand what would happen to ours.

For the first few days, we didn’t have anyone we could reliably turn to for merge request approvals, releases, or direction. The remaining manager in the US offered to help with approvals, but he was already busy and unfamiliar with our day-to-day work.

By Friday, we still didn’t really know what would happen next. People who’d been through something similar told me it could take a month before I was reassigned to a new team, which seemed like the most likely outcome at the time.

It’s been hard to make progress. I feel bad when work isn’t moving forward, even though there are also things I can’t move forward on my own.

## Meeting my mentees

Outside those changes, I started meeting my mentees through the mentoring program. They’re university students from Tamaulipas, and they’re excited to participate because they haven’t had professional experience yet.

One of them told me he’d like to develop a personal project with my guidance. I’m excited about that too; it gives us something concrete to work on together.

## My first mock interviews

I also conducted two online mock interviews this week. There are things I could have done better, but I’m learning through the process too.

I have another one scheduled for Monday, and I hope it will go more smoothly now that I’ve had some practice.

For now, I’m doing what I can with the work available, keeping my teammates updated, and helping where possible. I hope next week feels a little better.
