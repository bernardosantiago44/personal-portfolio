This was a quieter week. Workwise, I finally closed another ticket after an unfortunate series of events, most of which were outside my control. I also checked in with my mentees on their technical skills, worked with them on their CVs, and got a new addition to my desk.

## Settling in with our new manager

My team and I have been getting along well with our new manager. He’s very nice to work with, although he’s understandably busy managing two teams at the same time.

He’s still getting familiar with what our team does. That can make things a little tricky when I’m also learning, but he’s supportive and helps us as much as he can.

## Checking in with my mentees

I’ve been following up with my mentees, and they’ve been improving. Alongside their technical progress, we’ve also been working on their CVs.

We now have a talk scheduled about systems, networks, and security. It wasn’t originally on the schedule, but several mentees showed interest, so we managed to organize one. I’m planning to join as well; it’s a topic I know I can learn more about.

## Building my own corner of the web

This week, I also got my personal website running. For now, it presents my CV as a webpage. My next step is to add these blog entries. Hopefully, it’ll be hosted online next week, and I can start migrating my previous entries there.

I’m building it with Astro, which feels like a great fit for a site focused on static content. I like its fast loading times and the flexibility to work with components from other frameworks.

What I’m most excited about is its support for `.mdx` files. I can write in Markdown, bring in components when they’re useful, and use a shared layout to keep every entry looking consistent. That makes the idea of maintaining my own blog feel much more manageable.

I can even add code blocks:
```js
const a = 5;
const b = 10;
console.log(a+b); // 15
```

I’m excited to share it once it’s ready.
