<ProjectSection title="Problem" eyebrow="01">
  Small crypto investors often **track their holdings manually** across spreadsheets, exchanges, and price-tracking apps.
  This creates several issues:
  1. Portfolio data is **fragmented**.
  1. Price movements are **easy to miss**.
  1. Alerts are usually **limited** to exchange-specific assets.
  1. Users need a **lightweight tool** focused on their own holdings, not a full trading platform.

  The goal of this project is to provide a focused portfolio tracker where users can register their crypto holdings, analyze their portfolio, and receive automatic notifications when relevant market conditions occur.
